# বিদ্যুৎ লাইভ (Biddut Live)

**A live map of Bangladesh showing where the lights are on... and where they are not.**

🔗 **Live:** https://alnahian2003.github.io/biddut

---

## ⚠️ Read This First

**This is a satire project.** Every number, dot, and update on this map is made up. It is a humorous take on the electricity crisis — not real data, not affiliated with any power company or government body, and definitely not a reliable source for checking if your home has electricity. For that, please continue using the traditional method: flipping a light switch.

---

## 🤔 What Is This?

Imagine a weather forecast, but instead of rain, it tracks **load-shedding** across Bangladesh in "real time."

Open the map and you will see the whole country glowing red (no electricity) with a few lucky green spots (electricity exists!). Watch the live feed as power dramatically comes and goes, second by second, just like real life — except this one admits it's fake.

## ✨ What Can You Do With It?

- **🗺️ Explore the live map** — Zoom around Bangladesh and see which areas are "suffering" and which are "blessed."
- **💚 Spot the lucky zones** — Areas with electricity literally glow, like tiny beacons of hope in the darkness.
- **🔍 Search your area** — Type your neighborhood name (for example: মিরপুর ১০, গুলশান, স্বরূপকাঠি) and jump straight to it.
- **📡 Watch the live feed** — A running commentary of power cuts and restorations, updated every few seconds.
- **📋 Browse the directory** — A full list of areas, sorted by division, so you can check on your hometown.
- **🌙 Day and night mode** — Dark mode makes the whole country look as gloomy as a load-shedding evening should.

## 🚀 How Do I Open It?

No installation. No accounts. No technical knowledge required.

1. Download the project (click the green **Code** button on GitHub, then **Download ZIP**).
2. Unzip the folder.
3. Double-click the file named **`index.html`**.

It opens in your web browser, just like any website. That's it. You're done.

## 🎭 Is the Data Real?

**No. Not even a little bit.**

The locations are real places in Bangladesh, and the map border is accurate — but the power status of every area is randomly generated for entertainment. If your area shows green on this map but your fan is not spinning, trust the fan.

## ❤️ Why Does This Exist?

Because sometimes the best way to cope with a shared national struggle is to laugh at it together. This project turns the collective experience of load-shedding into something you can watch, explore, and chuckle at.
